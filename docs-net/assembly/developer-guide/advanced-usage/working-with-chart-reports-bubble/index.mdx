## The Bubble Chart

As per wikipedia, a Bubble Chart can be described as:

> A **bubble chart** is a type of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chart" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">chart</a> that displays three dimensions of data. Each entity with its triplet (*v*<sub>1</sub>, *v*<sub>2</sub>, *v*<sub>3</sub>) of associated data is plotted as a disk that expresses two of the *v*<sub>i</sub> values through the disk's *xy* location and the third through its size. Bubble charts can facilitate the understanding of social, economical, medical, and other scientific relationships.

For more information visit <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_chart" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">wikipedia article</a>.
