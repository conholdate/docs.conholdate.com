> Runnable C# examples for protection & signatures with Aspose.Slides for .NET.
> 0

Choose a task below. Each example includes a complete project, reproducible input, and checks of its saved result.

- [Open and Encrypt Presentations](/net/slides/examples/protection-signatures/passwords/): Open an encrypted presentation with a known password and save encrypted or unencrypted copies.
- [Set Editing Protection](/net/slides/examples/protection-signatures/write-protection/): Set and remove edit-password protection and read-only recommendations while distinguishing them from encryption.
- [Sign and Verify Presentations](/net/slides/examples/protection-signatures/digital-signatures/): Sign a synthetic presentation with a disposable test certificate, then detect a changed signed copy.
- [Protect Exported PDF Files](/net/slides/examples/protection-signatures/pdf-security/): Set a PDF opening password and requested printing permissions during presentation export.
