> Runnable C# examples for fonts & text processing with Aspose.Slides for .NET.
> 0

Choose a task below. Each example includes a complete project, reproducible input, and checks of its saved result.

- [Find and Replace Text Across a Deck](/net/slides/examples/fonts-text/find-replace/): Replace literal and regular-expression matches across slides, groups, tables, masters and notes.
- [Replace Presentation Fonts](/net/slides/examples/fonts-text/replace-fonts/): Replace an explicit font reference across a presentation and inspect the saved result.
- [Load Fonts and Configure Fallback](/net/slides/examples/fonts-text/custom-fonts/): Load application-managed fonts, configure substitution and character-range fallback, and collect export warnings.
- [Embed Fonts in a Presentation](/net/slides/examples/fonts-text/embed-fonts/): Embed a permitted font in full or with used characters, then reopen and render without its external folder.
- [Process Multilingual Text](/net/slides/examples/fonts-text/multilingual-text/): Render Arabic, mixed-direction text and wrapped Chinese text with explicit script-appropriate fonts.
