:::info
MS Excel supports setting different headers and footers for the first page, odd pages, and even pages since Excel 2007.\
Aspose.Cells supports the same feature.
:::

## Setting Different Headers and Footers in MS Excel {#setting-different-headers-and-footers-in-ms-excel}

**![Setting Different Headers and Footers](/net/cells/images/7998d0af3203fcc7-difpage.png)**

1. Click **Page Layout &gt; Print Titles &gt; Header/Footer**.
2. Check **Different Odd and Even Pages** or **Different First Page**.
3. Enter different headers and footers.

## Setting Different Headers and Footers with Aspose.Cells {#setting-different-headers-and-footers-with-asposecells}

Aspose.Cells behaves the same as Excel.

1. Set the flags <a href="https://reference.aspose.com/cells/net/aspose.cells/pagesetup/ishfdiffoddeven/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">PageSetup.IsHFDiffOddEven</a> and <a href="https://reference.aspose.com/cells/net/aspose.cells/pagesetup/IsHFDiffFirst/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">PageSetup.IsHFDiffFirst</a>.
2. Enter different headers and footers.

```csharp
//Creates a new file.
var wb = new Workbook();
//Gets the setting of page setup.
PageSetup pageSetup = wb.Worksheets[0].PageSetup;
//Sets different odd and even pages
pageSetup.IsHFDiffOddEven = true;
pageSetup.SetHeader(1, "I am the header of the Odd page.");
pageSetup.SetEvenHeader(1, "I am  the header of the Even page.");
//Sets different first page
pageSetup.IsHFDiffFirst = true;
pageSetup.SetFirstPageHeader(1, "I am  the header of the First page.");
```
